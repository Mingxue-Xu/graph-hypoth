# Claims Before Causes: Why a Literature-Grounded Hypothesis Pipeline Implements a Claim Graph Rather Than a Causal Graph

*Complete report. A four-page decision version is at [claims-before-causes-decision.md](claims-before-causes-decision.md).*

## Abstract

GraphHypothesize constructs a *claim graph*: a directed graph whose edges are natural-language causal assertions carrying provenance, evidence links, deterministic verdicts and scalar confidence, assembled by a multi-agent pipeline under an append-only transactional core. This report answers three questions. First, why does the system implement a causal *claim* graph rather than a causal graph in the sense of Pearl's structural causal models, what does that choice solve and leave unsolved, and what is gained? Second, what are the strengths and weaknesses of the implementation as it stands? Third, what conditions would have to hold before a strict causal graph became implementable? Drawing on the public source tree, shipped defaults, public documentation, and the 1989–2026 methodological literature, we argue that the claim-graph choice is not a provisional shortcut but the only representation whose truth-conditions the system can honestly enforce. The preconditions of a genuine causal graph, which are well-defined variables and interventions, causal sufficiency, Markov and faithfulness assumptions, and data against which they could be tested, are unsatisfiable for knowledge extracted from literature by imperfect extractors, and neither statistical causal discovery nor LLM-based causal judgment bridges the gap. We also ask why a hypothesis pipeline should carry causal framing and deterministic settlement at all, when contemporary hypothesis generators and their benchmarks do without either. The implemented design gives engineering answers rather than epistemic ones: the pipeline commits durable state, so it needs a deterministic commit boundary; support can be measured only on decomposed, typed claims, and the questions asked of each claim are causal; the deliverable is an experiment, which is a causal object; committed decisions must not flip between runs, as model judgments do; and the output moves resources, so abstention is rational. None of this shows that the machinery yields better hypotheses, and we say so. We identify a load-bearing configuration gap that narrows the shipped verdict space to support-or-abstain, give a seven-condition ladder from ontology-grounded variables to closed intervention loops that would license the promotion of claims to causes, and trace the argument through one production hypothesis. We conclude that the correct trajectory is not to convert the claim graph into a causal graph but to certify a derived causal layer above it, context by context, with promotions recorded as transactions.

## 1. Introduction

Systems that mine scientific literature for causal knowledge face a foundational representational choice. They may build a *causal graph*: a directed acyclic graph over variables which, under the semantics of structural causal models (SCMs), supports reasoning about interventions and counterfactuals <sup>[1](#reference-1), [2](#reference-2)</sup>. Or they may build a *claim graph*: a graph whose edges record that particular sources assert causal relations, together with the evidence, provenance and appraisal of those assertions. The two objects look alike when drawn; they differ in kind. The first is ontic: its edges purport to describe mechanisms in the world, and their truth-conditions involve what would happen under intervention. The second is epistemic: its edges describe the state of a literature, and their truth-conditions involve accurate extraction, faithful provenance and sound appraisal.

GraphHypothesize deliberately implements the second object. Its terminology is explicit: a claim graph is "a directed graph in which nodes represent variables or concepts and each directed edge *asserts* a causal relation from source to target; edges carry their supporting evidence and, once verified, a verdict." Its README makes the same commitment: concepts are nodes, causal assertions are edges, and evidence, confidence, open risks and experiment plans remain attached to the claims they qualify. Code identifiers call this object a *causal claim graph*; the documentation now uses the shorter *claim graph*, attaching the qualifier *causal* to the claims rather than to the graph, and this report follows that usage.

The analysis rests on three evidence bases. First, the public implementation: the durable object model (`src/graph_store.py`), the transaction core (`src/delta.py`, `src/validator.py`, `src/transaction_log.py`), and every pipeline cycle (extraction, priority, association, verification, hypothesis expansion, critic panel, experiment design). Second, the shipped configuration defaults and public guides that define the runnable workflow. Third, the methodological literature on causal graphs, causal discovery, LLM causal reasoning, literature-derived knowledge bases and evidence synthesis, from Cartwright's 1989 argument to ACL 2025 and CLeaR 2025 results. For each load-bearing claim the report states the specific evidence in the original source from which it derives; external figures are reported as read and were not independently reproduced.

Section 2 assembles the formal requirements a genuine causal graph imposes. Section 3 characterises what the system builds. Section 4 answers Question 1: why a strict causal graph is not implementable here, what the claim-graph approach has and has not solved, and why causal claims and deterministic settlement are worth carrying at all. Section 5 appraises the implementation (Question 2). Section 6 states the conditions under which a strict causal graph would become implementable (Question 3). Section 7 grounds the argument in a case study. Sections 8 to 10 situate the system, state limitations and conclude.

## 2. What a causal graph formally requires

### 2.1 Structural causal models and the do-calculus

In the SCM framework a causal graph is not a picture of associations but a commitment to a family of interventional distributions. Each edge X→Y asserts that Y's structural equation depends on X, and the graph as a whole licenses the computation of quantities such as P(Y | do(X)) <sup>[1](#reference-1)</sup>. The do-calculus, three graphical rewrite rules over interventional expressions, was proved complete for identification by Huang and Valtorta <sup>[3](#reference-3)</sup> and, for conditional interventional distributions, by Shpitser and Pearl <sup>[4](#reference-4)</sup>: every identifiable interventional quantity can be derived by the three rules, and failure of the rules implies genuine non-identifiability. The completeness results are powerful precisely because they are conditional. Everything is relative to a correct causal diagram over well-defined variables; Pearl's own retrospective is explicit that the calculus operates "given the assumptions encoded in the diagram" <sup>[5](#reference-5)</sup>. An edge whose origin is a sentence in a paper carries none of the modularity and invariance semantics the calculus consumes. Applying do-calculus to it is a category error until the underlying assumptions have been argued.

### 2.2 The assumption base

Constraint-based and score-based causal discovery, as codified by Spirtes, Glymour and Scheines <sup>[2](#reference-2)</sup>, requires at minimum the causal Markov condition (each variable is independent of its non-descendants given its parents), faithfulness (all and only the independencies implied by the graph hold in the distribution), usually causal sufficiency (the measured set contains all common causes of any two measured variables), and acyclicity. None is conferred by drawing arrows. Faithfulness in particular is a substantive empirical assumption with well-analysed failure modes: Zhang distinguishes three logically distinct components of it, only some of which are testable even in principle <sup>[6](#reference-6)</sup>. Causal sufficiency is untestable from within the measured set, and its violation, latent confounding, is the default condition of observational science.

### 2.3 Well-defined variables and interventions

Hernán and Robins add the epidemiologist's precondition: causal contrasts require well-defined interventions <sup>[7](#reference-7)</sup>. The consistency assumption, that the observed outcome under the treatment actually received equals the counterfactual outcome under that treatment, is meaningful only when "treatment" picks out a sufficiently specific manipulation; "the effect of obesity" is ill-posed until one says whether the intervention is diet, surgery or exercise. How often applied research meets this bar was recently audited: a 2025 review in the *International Journal of Epidemiology* found the well-defined-intervention assumption routinely left unevaluated <sup>[8](#reference-8)</sup>. Literature-extracted claims of the form "stress causes inflammation" fail this precondition by construction, since the sentence does not specify the intervention version of its own cause variable. A parallel constraint arises from causal representation learning and causal abstraction theory: causal variables are generally not given in raw observations <sup>[9](#reference-9)</sup>, and whether an aggregate or text-derived concept behaves as a single causal variable is a formal property, the existence of an exact transformation between micro- and macro-models, that must be established rather than assumed <sup>[10](#reference-10), [11](#reference-11)</sup>.

### 2.4 Philosophical constraints

Cartwright's slogan "no causes in, no causes out" summarises her argument that no algorithm produces causal conclusions from associational input without prior causal assumptions <sup>[12](#reference-12)</sup>. A claim graph supplies "causes in" only in an epistemic sense, since people asserted causes, which is not the structural input the theorems need. Russo and Williamson add a discipline-level constraint from the health sciences: causal claims are established there by the joint presence of difference-making evidence (statistical dependence under variation) and mechanistic evidence (a plausible pathway); neither kind alone suffices, and a count of assertions of either kind certainly does not <sup>[13](#reference-13)</sup>. Together these fix the bar that any strict causal graph must clear.

## 3. The system under analysis

### 3.1 Object model: assertions with settlement states

The durable state is a single store, `CausalClaimGraphStore`, holding concept nodes, causal edges, evidence links, scope context and experiment plans. The node type is documented in-code as "an inspectable semantic atom with no status; status belongs to edges." The edge type is "a typed directed causal assertion" that begins life `unverified`. Its epistemic vocabulary is a closed status enum (`unverified`, `supported`, `contradicted`, `qualified`, `insufficient`, `not_causal`) plus a scalar confidence and free-text fields for mechanism, conditions and confounders. Identity is content-addressed: a node ID hashes the normalised label and definition; an edge ID hashes endpoints, direction string and relation type. The edge hash ignores mechanism, status and confidence, and polarity, effect size, dose and population appear nowhere as typed fields.

### 3.2 Transactional core and deterministic authority

Every durable mutation travels as a typed delta proposal in one of five families (extract, priority, verify, hypothesis, experiment) and passes five deterministic gates, which are payload well-formedness, referential integrity, base-hash currency, status-transition legality and idempotency, before the transaction log applies it and appends an auditable row. Rejected proposals leave state byte-identical and still produce a row. The central runtime boundary is a strict division of authority: model-backed roles may propose structure, appraise passages and draft plans, but deterministic code owns evidence strength, confidence and verdict selection; no model response is treated as a durable verdict, transaction acceptance or graph mutation without the relevant deterministic boundary. Researcher priority is stored separately and cannot contribute to a causal verdict, confidence value, evidence signal or candidate eligibility.

### 3.3 Evidence verification and confidence

Verification is the system's epistemic heart. Retrieved passages are triaged by a deterministic association score; a single Evidence Reviewer call grades each passage on clamped [0, 1] sub-signals (entailment per relation label, context fit, construct match, contradiction risk; design strength, confounder adjustment, measurement quality, population match, bias risk), under an explicit prompt rule that the reviewer "states no conclusion, verdict, or label." Fixed arithmetic then computes per-passage relation labels and strengths, and per-verdict confidence:

```
Conf(v) = clip01( 0.55·Top3 + 0.25·Balance + 0.20·Breadth − 0.25·OpenRisk − 0.15·Limits )
```

where Top3 is the mean of up to the three strongest supporting passages, Balance weighs support against counterevidence strength, Breadth counts distinct sources capped at three, and OpenRisk and Limits are capped counts of unresolved risks and qualifying limitations. Verdict selection is deterministic with two abstention rules: `insufficient` is returned when no candidate clears a 0.5 confidence floor, or when the top two candidates lie within a near-tie margin of 0.025. An *overclaim guard* refuses to commit any verdict whose required evidence role (for example a contradiction-role passage for a `contradict` verdict) was never present, returning no transaction rather than a hidden failure.

### 3.4 Expansion, critique and experiment design

Hypothesis generation runs as a persistent Synthesist thread (mine, propose, revise) whose candidates are graded by a two-critic panel, structurally blinded to the proposer's rationale, with a third judge consulted only on disagreement; aggregation is deterministic (median grades, majority flags, Borda rank fusion). Candidates without a mechanism chain and verbatim source quotes are dropped at parse time. Nothing enters the graph without explicit confirmation. Each confirmed hypothesis then receives an experiment plan: design type from a five-value enum, operationalisation, intervention and comparator prose, controls drawn from the graph's confounder-, mediator- and moderator-typed neighbour nodes, metrics with predicted directions, and expected *and falsifying* outcomes, grounded in a targeted methods-retrieval batch under the rule that citations must resolve within the exact supplied passage set or be declared an explicit empty grounding (`no_relevant_methods` or `no_methods_retrieved`), never fabricated.

### 3.5 Epistemic characterisation

Assembled, the system is a provenance-and-settlement ledger over natural-language causal assertions of the form "source S, in context C, asserts X→Y, with this provenance, appraised this way." Searching the source tree for intervention machinery confirms the negative space: there is no do-operator, no structural equation, no adjustment-set computation, no d-separation, no acyclicity constraint (A→B and B→A can co-exist), and no identifiability check of any kind. The only place study-design concepts appear (randomisation, instrumental variables, GRADE-style quality) is inside the Evidence Reviewer's prompt, as properties of what a passage reports, graded by a model and weighted at 0.45 of per-passage strength. The system's causal vocabulary is entirely second-order. It is about the literature's claims, not about mechanisms, and Section 4 argues this is exactly right.

## 4. Question 1: why a claim graph, not a causal graph

### 4.1 The preconditions fail, severally and jointly

Measured against Section 2, a literature-fed pipeline fails every precondition of a strict causal graph. **Variables.** Nodes are content-addressed text labels with an open-string type field, not ontology-grounded quantities with measurement semantics. By the causal-abstraction results, even well-curated aggregate concepts must be shown to behave as causal macro-variables <sup>[10](#reference-10), [11](#reference-11)</sup>; by Hernán and Robins and the 2025 audit, most literature claims never specify their intervention version <sup>[7](#reference-7), [8](#reference-8)</sup>. **Assumptions.** Causal sufficiency, Markov and faithfulness are properties of a distribution relative to a graph; the pipeline holds passages, not distributions, so none can be tested even in principle within the system. Faithfulness's partial untestability is a theorem-level result <sup>[6](#reference-6)</sup>, and latent confounding is the expected condition of the observational studies most claims summarise <sup>[2](#reference-2)</sup>. **Data.** There is no data plane: no dataset ingestion, no statistics service, no conditional-independence testing. Without data, even the discovery algorithms of Section 4.4 have nothing to operate on.

### 4.2 The extractors are unreliable at exactly this task

The field-scale evidence sets the noise regime. SemRep, the extractor behind SemMedDB, the largest biomedical predication store <sup>[15](#reference-15)</sup>, achieves 0.69 precision and 0.42 recall under relaxed evaluation (0.55 and 0.34 strict), with named-entity recognition and normalisation the single largest error source at roughly 27% of errors <sup>[14](#reference-14)</sup>. CauseNet, the web-scale analogue, reports about 83% extraction precision over eleven million relations and is accordingly framed by its authors as a graph of "claimed causal relations" <sup>[16](#reference-16)</sup>. The project's own local-model analysis reaches the same conclusion from the causal-language literature: on PubMedCausal, detecting that a paragraph contains causal language and extracting the exact cause–effect pair are different tasks with very different scores, and a fine-tuned extractor collapsed from 87% to 40% under domain shift (see [Which Agents Go Local?](which-agents-go-local.md)). A graph built at 0.7 precision is a defensible claim record with per-edge verification downstream; as a causal structure it would be wrong nearly one edge in three before any inferential error is added.

### 4.3 Language models do not close the gap

Could a frontier model supply the missing causal judgment? The 2023–2025 record says no, in a specific and instructive way. Kıcıman et al. found LLMs strong at knowledge-based causal tasks, with up to 97% pairwise direction accuracy on the Tübingen cause–effect pairs, while cautioning that failure modes are unpredictable and positioning LLMs as assistants for setting up analyses rather than as inference engines <sup>[17](#reference-17)</sup>. Zečević et al. supplied the diagnosis: LLMs are "causal parrots," reciting a meta-SCM of causal facts as talked about in training text rather than performing inference <sup>[18](#reference-18)</sup>. The diagnosis has since been corroborated three ways. Contamination: GPT-4 can reproduce the Tübingen benchmark itself, recalling roughly 61% of its cells, so benchmark wins cannot evidence reasoning <sup>[19](#reference-19)</sup>. Controlled inference: on Corr2Cause, where causal conclusions must be derived from stated correlational premises and cannot be memorised, none of seventeen evaluated models performed well, and fine-tuned gains collapsed under mere variable renaming <sup>[20](#reference-20)</sup>. Mechanism: an ACL 2025 audit tied causal-discovery performance directly to the frequency of the relation in pretraining text, with poor generalisation to rare relations <sup>[21](#reference-21)</sup>, and a companion line documents systematic causal fallacies such as inferring causation from temporal order <sup>[22](#reference-22)</sup>. The architectural implication is sharp. An LLM-authored "causal graph" would in fact be a claim graph, the model's compressed memory of the literature's assertions, with the provenance stripped off. Keeping the assertions explicit, sourced and auditable dominates it.

### 4.4 Statistical discovery would not rescue it either

The remaining route, running causal discovery over data and using claims only as priors, is the principled one (Section 6), but its current state cannot underwrite a causal graph of literature breadth. Reisach et al. showed that the celebrated performance of continuous structure learners on simulated additive-noise benchmarks is largely an artefact of varsortability, marginal variances increasing along the causal order in simulation, such that a trivial variance-sorting baseline competes with the learners and performance collapses when data are standardised <sup>[23](#reference-23)</sup>. Kaiser and Sipos showed NOTEARS is not even scale-invariant: re-expressing a variable in different units changes the recovered graph <sup>[24](#reference-24)</sup>. Nastl and Hardt tested the downstream promise on sixteen real tabular tasks and found that predictors using all features beat causal-feature predictors in and out of domain, with discovery algorithms either failing to run or selecting variables that performed no better; these results concern prediction under domain shift, not structure recovery, but they remove one practical argument for causal feature selection <sup>[25](#reference-25)</sup>. The field's own CLeaR 2025 self-assessment concedes reliance on unrealistic assumptions, toy synthetic evaluation and the near-absence of real-world ground truth <sup>[26](#reference-26)</sup>, and constraint-based methods err systematically under assumption violations and small samples <sup>[27](#reference-27)</sup>. None of this says discovery is hopeless. It says a system without data, without harmonised variables and without interventional access has no working algorithmic bridge from claims to causes today.

### 4.5 Aggregating claims is not causal inference

A tempting fallback is to treat the volume and agreement of literature claims as a proxy for causal truth. The bibliometric evidence forecloses it. Greenberg reconstructed the complete citation network around a single biomedical belief, 242 papers linked by 675 citations expanding to 220,553 citation paths, and documented citation bias against refuting studies, amplification through papers containing no data, and "invention": hypothesis transmuted into established fact by citation alone, with the distortions propagating into grant applications <sup>[28](#reference-28)</sup>. Duyx et al.'s meta-analysis of 52 studies quantified the field-general gradient: statistically significant results are cited about 1.6 times more, and papers claiming support for their hypothesis about 2.7 times more, than their counterparts <sup>[29](#reference-29)</sup>; de Vries et al. traced how reporting and citation biases compound through the evidence pipeline for antidepressant trials <sup>[30](#reference-30)</sup>. Evidence-synthesis methodology has internalised the lesson. Significance-based vote counting should be avoided, and direction-of-effect vote counting with a sign test is a limited option when only effect direction is available, yet synthesis without meta-analysis remains common and seldom specified <sup>[31](#reference-31)</sup>. GRADE instead rates certainty in a body of evidence for an outcome along risk-of-bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication-bias dimensions <sup>[32](#reference-32)</sup>, against the base-rate warning of Ioannidis <sup>[33](#reference-33)</sup>; applying its domains to individual graph assertions would be a project-specific adaptation. "N papers assert this edge" is therefore a social signal about the literature, exactly what a claim graph honestly records, and nothing more. This is Cartwright's dictum in operational form <sup>[12](#reference-12)</sup>.

### 4.6 Claim-native precedents point the same way

The relevant external evidence forms an argument for the claim-level representation. Its strongest cluster is uniformly claim-native: the FActScore lineage, which evaluates long-form generation by decomposing it into atomic, individually verifiable claims scored against sources <sup>[34](#reference-34)</sup>, with successors that verify each atomic fact retrieval by retrieval (AFEV <sup>[35](#reference-35)</sup>) and score the quality of the decomposition itself (DecMetrics <sup>[36](#reference-36)</sup>), while SciEx notes that no gold benchmark yet exists for complete atomic-claim extraction from scientific text <sup>[37](#reference-37)</sup>. POPPER validates free-form hypotheses by agentic sequential falsification, aggregating p-values into e-values under strict Type-I error control, which is belief semantics over natural-language claims with no DAG anywhere <sup>[38](#reference-38)</sup>. Kosmos, the strongest structured-state result among AI-scientist systems, coordinates parallel agent rollouts through a shared world model of cited statements, with independent scientists finding 79.4% of its report statements accurate <sup>[39](#reference-39)</sup>. Heuer's Analysis of Competing Hypotheses contributes the decision layer: rival hypotheses scored against an evidence matrix, refutation-first, weighting evidence by diagnosticity <sup>[40](#reference-40)</sup>. Meanwhile the formal alternatives are cited with their blocking preconditions attached. Likelihood-free Bayesian model discrimination requires specified candidate generative models, priors, a design objective and efficient simulators for every candidate <sup>[41](#reference-41)</sup>, which free-form scientific hypotheses lack; BED-LLM's expected-information-gain machinery optimises a single variable of interest, not discrimination among rival mechanistic explanations <sup>[42](#reference-42)</sup>; and Robin is cited for its caveat that free-form agent analysis of its own experiments required human re-verification <sup>[43](#reference-43)</sup>, which supports deterministic verdict rules. Every validated building block available here operates at the claim-plus-evidence-plus-confidence level; the formally superior machinery is blocked by preconditions the domain cannot meet.

### 4.7 What is solved, what is not, and what was gained

**Solved.** The claim-graph commitment solves, first, the honesty problem: the stored object's truth-conditions (faithful extraction, real provenance, sound appraisal, disclosed abstention) are ones the system can enforce, and it enforces them structurally, through deterministic verdict authority, fail-closed parsers, the overclaim guard, abstention on floor and near-tie, and a reader-facing card hard-coded to say "This is a testable idea, not a proven conclusion." Second, the audit problem: content-addressed identity, five-gate transactions, receipts, and batch-scoped evidence resolution that renders "unresolved" rather than guessing a source make every edge's history reconstructible, the property Kosmos's authors found necessary for multi-agent coherence <sup>[39](#reference-39)</sup> and INDRA institutionalised for literature assembly <sup>[45](#reference-45)</sup>. Third, the authority-contamination problem: researcher priority is authored, never inferred, and firewalled out of verdicts; proposer, critic, designer and validator are separated roles, so no single model output is authoritative. Cache-key columns are authoritative over metadata, quote resolution fails closed, linked evidence IDs must resolve, and hypotheses commit only on explicit confirmation.

**Not solved, by design and by gap.** By design: identification. A `supported` verdict at confidence 0.8 summarises literature appraisal; it estimates no interventional quantity and licenses no do-calculus step. By gap: the shipped configuration schedules only the support evidence role, structurally foreclosing the contradiction-bearing verdicts (Section 5.3); causal content remains non-computable prose, and contradictory literature is often context underspecification the graph cannot index <sup>[44](#reference-44)</sup>; citation and publication bias enter the evidence stream unmodelled, with breadth capped at three distinct sources; and an open loop to experiments, since plans are produced but none is executed or fed back.

**Gained.** A durably exported, versioned graph state backed during execution by a replay-capable transaction log, which a conversation transcript or a model's parametric memory can never be; exact transaction replay requires retention of the full transaction rows and the external model and retrieval inputs. Containment guarantees convert unreliable model outputs into recorded, bounded inputs. And the state already carries the fields a future causal layer needs: mechanism text, confounder and mediator nodes, scope qualifiers and plan objects. No integrated system in the surveyed literature yet takes a literature-derived claim graph up that ladder; a provenance-preserving claim graph is the state of the art (INDRA <sup>[45](#reference-45)</sup>, Kosmos <sup>[39](#reference-39)</sup>) and the precondition for attempting it.

### 4.8 Why causal claims and deterministic measurement at all?

Contemporary hypothesis generators carry neither commitment and appear none the worse for it. MOOSE-Chem <sup>[46](#reference-46)</sup> and ResearchBench <sup>[47](#reference-47)</sup> rediscover published hypotheses that a model judge scores for similarity to the ground truth; ResearchAgent is graded by reviewer personas <sup>[48](#reference-48)</sup>; HypoBench and its HypoGeniC and HypoRefine lineage score natural-language hypotheses by held-out predictive accuracy <sup>[49](#reference-49), [50](#reference-50), [51](#reference-51)</sup>; TruthHypo scores them against relations that later entered a knowledge graph <sup>[52](#reference-52)</sup>; and the co-scientist ranks free-text hypotheses by an Elo tournament of model debates <sup>[53](#reference-53)</sup>. None builds a typed causal graph, keeps a provenance ledger, fuses verdicts deterministically or abstains, and the project's own evaluation protocol, `fhq-v1`, will compare GraphHypothesize with several of them on TOMATO-Chem by blinded expert preference. This section asks the prior question directly, from the visible implementation choices and the literatures they draw on. Five reasons emerge. They are reasons about state, measurement and cost, not claims that causal framing or deterministic settlement produces better hypotheses; the closing paragraph says what the evidence does not show.

**1. The pipeline commits durable state, and a commit boundary has to be deterministic.** The public implementation makes the durable product a causal claim graph plus transaction log. Agents propose or verify graph deltas; transaction validation, not a model controller, decides what persists. This is the generate-then-verify pattern, and its evidence is strong exactly where the check reduces to a structural or executable property: a symbolic deduction engine solving 25 of 30 olympiad geometry problems <sup>[54](#reference-54)</sup>, executed tests raising HumanEval pass@1 from 47.0% to 65.8% <sup>[55](#reference-55)</sup>, and the LLM-Modulo position that models generate and external verifiers decide <sup>[56](#reference-56)</sup>. The five accept gates and verbatim quote matching are checks of this class. An idea generator commits nothing and so needs no commit boundary; GraphHypothesize commits every node, edge, verdict and plan into a versioned store that later cycles read, so it does. The failure it guards against is not hypothetical: in the MAST taxonomy of 1,600 multi-agent traces, no or incomplete verification (8.2%) and incorrect verification (9.1%) together account for 17.3% of annotated failures, and the broader category reaches 23.5% when premature termination is included <sup>[57](#reference-57)</sup>.

**2. Verifying a claim against literature requires decomposing it into typed targets, and the types are causal because the questions are.** Verification is target-scoped: retrieval and appraisal attach to one node or edge and one evidence role at a time, because the same paper can be strongly associated with one concept node, weakly with another, and differently relevant to a causal edge. Factuality evaluation and evidence synthesis converged on the same precondition: FActScore and SAFE score long-form text only after decomposing it into atomic, individually checkable claims, because support labels on whole passages are subjective <sup>[34](#reference-34), [58](#reference-58)</sup>, and GRADE grades certainty criterion by criterion rather than by impression <sup>[32](#reference-32)</sup>. What makes the decomposition causal rather than topical is the set of questions asked of each target. The evidence roles are support, contradiction, qualification, confounder and mechanism, and the verdict set includes `not_causal`, a label with no precedent in claim-verification datasets and kept as a deliberate product choice: the system is meant to record that a relation is associational but not causal, which a co-occurrence or topic graph cannot express. Typed, directional predications are also what let mechanistic knowledge compose and be checked for contradiction. Recasting Swanson's fish-oil and Raynaud discovery with SemRep's typed predicates recovered 14 of his 19 manual associations from 4,438 predications, because co-occurrence gives no insight into the nature of a relationship while "fish oil inhibits platelet aggregation; platelet aggregation causes Raynaud syndrome" does <sup>[59](#reference-59)</sup>; INDRA and EMMAA need polarity and direction because their outputs are executable models and automated contradiction detection, and their belief engine is deterministic code scoring noisy text-mined statements <sup>[45](#reference-45), [60](#reference-60)</sup>, the closest existing precedent for "deterministic code owns durable decisions." Composition is sound only when the shared node carries the same mechanism and context, which is why mechanism, conditions and scope live on the edge.

**3. The deliverable is an experiment, and an experiment is a causal object.** Woodward's definition of a direct cause, an intervention on X that changes Y while the other variables are held fixed by intervention, is nearly a template for the plan schema: intervention, comparator, controls, predicted and falsifying outcome <sup>[61](#reference-61)</sup>; Pearl's hierarchy states the same limit from the other side, that no quantity of associational data answers a do() question without an explicit causal model <sup>[62](#reference-62)</sup>. The intended user is a researcher mid-project: anomaly attribution among rival explanations requires the rivals to be causal so that discriminating experiments can be designed, with Heuer's diagnosticity as the scoring principle <sup>[40](#reference-40)</sup>; instrument validity becomes a verifiable claim that gates the trust placed in experimental outcomes; controls are drawn from confounder- and mediator-typed neighbours; and a researcher's own results return as evidence through the same verification path, closing a loop that only a typed graph can carry.

**4. Committed decisions must be invariant, and model judgments are not.** The runtime goal is committed-decision invariance, not bit-determinism. Bit-determinism of production model calls is unachievable, since the vendor exposes no seed and server-side batching leaves a documented residual flip rate at temperature zero, so the required guarantee is that the same input must not silently flip a committed decision across runs. Every gate, score and verdict is therefore a pure function of recorded, rubric-anchored ordinal sub-signals; the signals are recorded for replay; calibration work can estimate cross-run flip rates; near-ties abstain; and human confirmation is the terminal abstention. The defaults treat numbers as calibration proposals rather than truth, and quantification is confined to routing work, ranking evidence, validating graph transactions, and tracking cost and provenance. The literature on model judgment supports placing verdict authority outside the model: intrinsic self-correction does not improve reasoning <sup>[67](#reference-67)</sup>; judges are position-biased enough that reordering responses let a weaker model beat ChatGPT on 66 of 80 queries <sup>[68](#reference-68)</sup>; they detectably prefer their own generations <sup>[69](#reference-69)</sup>; and asked to find 91 author-confirmed errors in 83 papers, the best model recovered 21.1% of them at 6.1% precision and rarely found the same error twice <sup>[70](#reference-70)</sup>. Evidence synthesis reached the same posture for the same reason. Cochrane and PRISMA 2020 fix appraisal and analysis methods before results are seen because undisclosed post-hoc judgment is the documented route by which reviews drift <sup>[63](#reference-63), [64](#reference-64)</sup>; living reviews pre-specify the triggers for changing a conclusion so that a changed verdict is auditable rather than drift <sup>[65](#reference-65)</sup>, the analogue of a claim graph whose verdicts move as evidence accrues; and the 2025 Cochrane, Campbell, JBI and CEE position on AI requires disclosed model versions, prompts and verification steps <sup>[66](#reference-66)</sup>. Deterministic fusion over recorded judgments is what makes those norms satisfiable by a pipeline at all.

**5. The output moves resources, so precision and abstention are rational.** A plan the system emits may be executed. The cost asymmetry is large: pharmaceutical research productivity fell roughly 80-fold per inflation-adjusted dollar over six decades <sup>[71](#reference-71)</sup>, and irreproducible preclinical research is estimated at about $28 billion a year in the United States <sup>[72](#reference-72)</sup>. Under such asymmetry Chow's rule makes abstention rational whenever the cost of an error exceeds the cost of no answer <sup>[73](#reference-73)</sup>, while the default training incentive of language models rewards a confident wrong answer over an abstention <sup>[74](#reference-74)</sup>, so abstention has to be engineered, which is what the confidence floor, the near-tie hold and the overclaim guard do. The accountability regime points the same way: journal editors hold that an AI system cannot be an author because it cannot be accountable, and require a human able to trace every AI-assisted claim to its basis <sup>[75](#reference-75)</sup>, and reliance on outputs that cannot be checked produces "illusions of understanding" <sup>[76](#reference-76)</sup>. The counter-evidence bounds the scope rather than refuting the reason: Si et al. found blinded experts rate LLM-generated ideas more novel than human ones <sup>[77](#reference-77)</sup>, so precision machinery is defensible where a verdict or plan could commit resources, not at ideation, which is where the system places it, leaving the Synthesist free-form and gating verification and commit.

**Why the benchmarks have no such concern.** Every objective function in the benchmark set is a similarity, an accuracy or a preference computed once against a reference that already exists: a published paper, a labelled hold-out, a knowledge graph's later edges, a panel's taste. The question they ask is whether an output resembles or is preferred over a known item, never whether a specific claim, traceable to specific evidence, is supported, so they need no causal model, no evidence chain and no replay. No state is committed, so nothing needs a commit boundary; a wrong hypothesis costs a lower score, not a wasted experiment; and precision is almost never measured, with Kosmos's per-statement audit (79.4% overall, 57.9% on interpretive statements) the closest thing to one <sup>[39](#reference-39)</sup>. Where the concern does appear it sits in the evaluation layer: HypoBench and TruthHypo supply the data plane and the causal structure themselves, POPPER decides by a deterministic sequential test <sup>[38](#reference-38)</sup>, and `fhq-v1` freezes its protocol, blinds its judges and demotes same-family model judges to provisional. GraphHypothesize moves that discipline from the measurement of hypotheses into the product, because its product is a measurement.

**What the evidence does not show.** Three things. First, no result shows that causal typing improves hypothesis quality at the generation stage: Swanson's founding discoveries were made from title-word co-occurrence with the mechanism supplied by a human reader <sup>[78](#reference-78)</sup>, the strongest retrospective literature-based-discovery result came from embeddings over a partly typed graph <sup>[79](#reference-79)</sup>, and the systems whose hypotheses were later confirmed in the laboratory ranked by literature diffusion rather than by causal typing <sup>[80](#reference-80), [81](#reference-81)</sup>. The typing is justified by the deliverables, which are verifiable targets, contradiction detection and experiment plans, not by hit rate. Second, determinism reproduces arithmetic, not the reliability of its inputs: composite quality scores fed into otherwise rigorous meta-analyses can reverse their conclusions depending on the scale chosen <sup>[82](#reference-82)</sup>, the confidence formula is uncalibrated (Section 5.2), and the calibration work needed to estimate flip rates has not yet been reported. Reliability is necessary for validity, not sufficient. Third, the verdict space is presently starved to support-or-abstain (Section 5.3). The reasons above justify a design, not a result; the `fhq-v1` comparison and its ablation arm are where the design's effect on expert-preferred output will be measured, and a Kosmos-style statement audit is where its precision would be.

## 5. Question 2: appraisal of the current implementation

### 5.1 Strengths

- **Deterministic authority, versioned and replayable.** Confidence is a fixed formula over clamped sub-signals with calibration constants isolated in versioned defaults modules; the deterministic core replays from retained inputs, and the public docs state the reproducibility boundary honestly: model calls and live retrieval are recorded inputs, not replayable operations.
- **Abstention as a first-class outcome.** The 0.5 confidence floor, the 0.025 near-tie rule and the overclaim guard together implement a principled refusal to settle, rare among literature-mining systems, which typically emit their argmax.
- **Anti-spoofing provenance.** Cache-key columns are authoritative over evidence metadata, which "must not be able to override or forge the identity of its containing row"; quote verification requires near-exact source matches and rejects stitched quotes; ambiguous or unresolvable citations render as such; experiment plans must ground in the exact passage set supplied or declare an explicit empty grounding.
- **Structural role separation.** Critics graded blind to proposer rationale, a third judge only on disagreement, researcher confirmation gating hypothesis commits, priority firewalled from verdicts. These are Heuer's disciplines rendered as architecture <sup>[40](#reference-40)</sup>.
- **A contradiction-capable schema.** Verdicts for contradiction, qualification and non-causality, and evidence roles for contradiction, mechanism and confounder, exist and are wired through the fusion arithmetic and the transition rules, with settled statuses terminal and `insufficient` re-reviewable. Parsing fails closed throughout.

### 5.2 Weaknesses

- **Confidence is an uncalibrated scalar heuristic.** The weights are hand-set, validated against no ground truth, and conflate evidence quantity, quality and consistency in one number; it is not a probability of anything. A subtle trap compounds it: an edge settled `insufficient` durably carries the confidence of the rejected leading verdict, which a downstream consumer can misread.
- **Causal content is inert prose.** Polarity, effect size, dose, population and context have no typed fields; `conditions` and `confounders` are declared but never written by any producer; and because edge identity ignores mechanism, two contradictory mechanistic claims about the same endpoints collapse into one edge.
- **No ontology grounding.** Node identity is normalised-label hashing plus a model merge adjudicator, precisely the entity-normalisation failure mode that dominates SemRep's error budget <sup>[14](#reference-14)</sup> and that Gilda-class grounding services exist to fix <sup>[83](#reference-83)</sup>.
- **Bias-blind evidence stream.** No correction or even annotation for the citation and publication biases of Section 4.5; source trust tiers exist but contribute nothing to confidence; breadth saturates at three distinct sources.
- **Deferred hardening and bypasses.** Prompt-injection guards for retrieved text were deliberately removed from all graph-pipeline seams and deferred (per `nice-to-have.md`), while retrieved passages feed the graded signals; and `apply()` on the delta validator is a public method that skips all five gates for direct callers, documented but a standing footgun.
- **Minor soft spots.** Panel numeric grades are not clamped and unknown candidate IDs can enter Borda tallies; there is no cycle detection; batch-local evidence IDs invite mis-joins if batches are flattened; the scheduling helpers specified for iterative review are present but unused by the production path.

### 5.3 The reachability gap

One finding deserves its own subsection because it changes what the system *is* in practice. The default workflow schedules verification for the support role only (`src/run_path.py`, `verify_roles=(SUPPORT,)`), and neither shipped entry point overrides the default. Consequently every committed evidence link carries role support; the recorded counterevidence set is always empty; and any deterministically selected verdict of `contradict`, `qualify` or `not_causal` necessarily trips the overclaim guard, whose role requirements demand a contradiction- or confounder-role link the retrieval plan never created, and is withheld. The system discloses each withholding as an open risk, so nothing is hidden; but in every shipped run the verdict vocabulary collapses to support-or-abstain: the shipped configuration can commit only two of the five verification verdicts, supported and insufficient. The guard is doing its job; the retrieval schedule upstream is what starves it. Scheduling the contradiction, qualification, mechanism and confounder roles is thus the single highest-leverage change available: it would activate already-implemented machinery for exactly the verdicts, refutations and confounder explanations, that Section 4.5's evidence shows the citation record systematically under-supplies and that Heuer's diagnosticity principle identifies as the most informative <sup>[40](#reference-40)</sup>. Generated hypotheses additionally require a post-expansion retrieval, scheduling and verification pass, because expansion creates their edges after the workflow's only verification pass.

### 5.4 Net assessment

The implementation is unusually transparent for its genre: its guards refuse precisely the overclaims that literature-mining systems routinely commit, and its public docs and tests expose the remaining soft spots. Its two structural debts are epistemic range (the reachability gap) and epistemic depth (scalar, uncalibrated confidence over bias-uncorrected evidence). Neither debt undermines the representational choice; both are payable within it, and the [Research Agenda](research-agenda.md) develops them as its first two studies.

## 6. Question 3: conditions for a strict causal graph

"Impossible now" is accurate at literature scale, but it is not a mystical impossibility; it decomposes into seven conditions, ordered as a ladder because each presupposes those below it. In narrow regimes, such as a single laboratory, a few harmonised variables and a perturbable system like a pooled CRISPR or Perturb-seq screen, the modelling conditions are satisfiable today, which is why active-intervention methods succeed there <sup>[89](#reference-89)</sup>. For a graph spanning the open literature, none is yet fully met.

1. **Well-defined variables.** Every node grounded to a stable ontology identity with measurement semantics; the service model exists, since Gilda covers 1.8M names across nine ontologies with machine-learned disambiguation <sup>[83](#reference-83)</sup>. Each node must be defensible as a causal macro-variable under the abstraction criteria <sup>[10](#reference-10), [11](#reference-11)</sup> and every cause given its intervention version <sup>[7](#reference-7)</sup>. Locally this means ontology IDs, units and measurement fields on the concept node.
2. **Context-indexed edges.** Claims annotated with population, setting, dose and regime, with mergers across contexts decided by transportability theory, selection diagrams and the fusion rules of Bareinboim and Pearl <sup>[84](#reference-84)</sup>. The payoff is immediate: in an exploratory SemMedDB analysis restricted to twenty disorders and selected opposing predicates, contextual differences explained most of the fifty-eight apparent contradictions that survived manual filtering <sup>[44](#reference-44)</sup>.
3. **A data plane.** Observational micro-data attached per context, making conditional-independence structure testable and some faithfulness violations diagnosable, while faithfulness remains partly untestable because the true graph is unknown <sup>[6](#reference-6)</sup>. This is the largest single addition: dataset ingestion, harmonisation and a statistics service.
4. **Explicit, bounded structural assumptions.** Sufficiency argued or relaxed, since FCI-class algorithms tolerate latent confounders at the price of weaker, partially oriented output <sup>[2](#reference-2)</sup>; acyclicity asserted or replaced by explicit feedback semantics; and, the claim graph's native contribution, every such assumption recorded as its own auditable claim, so the causal layer's assumption ledger is itself provenance-tracked.
5. **Claims demoted to priors.** Literature edges become soft priors and constraints for structure learning over pooled observational and experimental regimes: Joint Causal Inference learns structure and intervention targets across contexts <sup>[85](#reference-85)</sup>, GIES exploits interventional Markov equivalence <sup>[86](#reference-86)</sup>, and the RCT-plus-observational fusion literature supplies estimator-level combination <sup>[87](#reference-87)</sup>, with LLMs confined to the extractor and critic roles the reliability evidence supports <sup>[17](#reference-17), [21](#reference-21)</sup>.
6. **A closed intervention loop.** Experiments selected to discriminate what observation cannot, by active learning of interventions with provably minimal experiment sets <sup>[88](#reference-88)</sup> or Bayesian acquisition over causal models validated on Perturb-CITE-seq <sup>[89](#reference-89)</sup>, executed, and fed back under statistical error control in POPPER's sequential sense <sup>[38](#reference-38)</sup>. The system already produces plan objects with falsifying outcomes; execution and feedback are the missing halves.
7. **Bias-corrected evidence semantics.** Before any edge is promoted, publication- and citation-bias modelling <sup>[28](#reference-28), [29](#reference-29)</sup>, GRADE-style multidimensional certainty in place of the single scalar <sup>[32](#reference-32)</sup>, and the Russo–Williamson requirement that both difference-making and mechanistic evidence be present <sup>[13](#reference-13)</sup>.

**Architectural consequence.** Conditions 1–6 concern the specification, evaluation and updating of a context-specific causal model; condition 7 is the project's additional evidential policy for promoting literature-derived assertions. The conditions do not counsel converting the claim graph; they counsel extending it. The epistemic layer should remain exactly what it is, the assertion record, the assumption ledger and the prior, while a derived causal layer exists only per context where conditions 1–6 are certified, condition 7 is required before promoting an edge, and each promotion from claim to cause is recorded as a transaction like any other mutation. This two-layer separation borrows the design of nanopublications, which separate an assertion graph from a provenance graph <sup>[90](#reference-90), [91](#reference-91)</sup>, and of INDRA, whose belief score is defined as the probability that a statement was correctly extracted and supported, deliberately not the probability that the causal relation holds <sup>[45](#reference-45)</sup>. The layering is the proven pattern; the ladder above it is what no system has yet climbed end to end.

A bounded first step short of the ladder is a task contract that distinguishes reporting what a source says, predicting an outcome, estimating an intervention effect, and transporting an effect to a new population. Bareinboim and Pearl formalise the relationship among causal queries, study designs, selection and transport across populations <sup>[84](#reference-84)</sup>, and Lelova, Cooper and Triantafillou propose a Bayesian method that uses target observational data and source experimental data to select adjustment sets without requiring the causal graph in advance <sup>[92](#reference-92)</sup>. Neither licenses causal inference from an arbitrary collection of natural-language edges. For a bounded causal task the contract would specify variables, units, target population, available observational or experimental data, assumptions and a designated identification or estimation method, and unknown requirements would remain unknown. Prediction under distribution shift and causal transportability are related deployment concerns with different mathematical requirements, and they should not be collapsed into a generic "domain similarity" score.

## 7. Case study: hypothesis h4 from a production run

The preceding sections argue in general; this section grounds the argument in a single concrete artifact. We examine hypothesis h4 from a live run of 31 August 2026 on the post-training LLM-compression literature, which covers low-rank factorisation, quantisation and their combination, anchored in the CALDERA decomposition (a quantised backbone plus a low-rank correction) <sup>[93](#reference-93)</sup> together with tensor-train and structured-pruning work. The final graph holds 25 nodes and 10 edges; all 20 recorded node-provenance entries are literature-mined, no researcher priority was authored, seven proposal transactions were accepted during the run, and a post-run repair pass recovered four experiment plans from the append-only audit log through four additional validated transactions, itself a small demonstration of the transaction core doing its job.

### 7.1 What the system produced

Hypothesis h4 conjectures, in its reader-facing headline, that "low-rank structure might reveal when low-precision storage adds more error to a compressed language model." Durably, it is a two-edge chain: *inherent low-rank structure of a weight matrix* → (`conditions`) → *rank–precision error coupling* → (`determines effectiveness of`) → *low-rank, low-precision decomposition*. Three details repay attention. First, provenance is discriminating: the two outer concepts carry literature-enrichment provenance resolving by verbatim quote match to the CALDERA paper, while the central construct carries an empty provenance list. The graph itself records that the load-bearing concept is the hypothesis's own invention, not a mined fact. Second, the critic panel's audit grades both mechanism steps `vague` ("CALDERA says it harnesses inherent low-rank structure, but does not establish an interaction between rank and precision errors"; "no passage asserts that a coupling determines reconstruction or task outcomes") and grades the coupling term `stretched`. Third, both edges are committed `unverified` with null confidence, and the run-level audit memo discloses why nothing in the run reached a verdict: "roles not scheduled: {contradiction, qualification, mechanism, confounder}", the reachability gap of Section 5.3, visible in production. The graph also shows zero evidence links: hypothesis edges carry no retrieved passages by design, since their grounding is deferred to the experiment stage, so h4's epistemic status rests entirely on the panel audit and the researcher's confirmation.

Attached to the first edge is a committed experiment plan of genuine methodological quality: a preregistered factorial rank-by-precision ablation on frozen weight matrices, with the coupling operationalised as the rank×precision interaction term of a paired factorial model of reconstruction error; matched rank-only and precision-only baselines at equal storage; three metrics with predicted directions (interaction coefficient, perplexity, task scores); five named control factors with handling rules; an expected outcome; and an explicit falsification clause ("refuted if strata and the continuous low-rank-structure measure do not predict the rank-by-precision interaction, or if joint decomposition outcomes are fully explained by separate rank-only and precision-only errors"). Equally telling is what the plan refuses. Its grounding status is `no_relevant_methods`: methods retrieval returned nothing citable, the Designer declared an explicitly empty grounding, and the rendered page labels the plan an "ungrounded draft" whose materials name no concrete model, dataset or benchmark. A sibling hypothesis in the same run achieved `grounded` status, confirming the distinction is live. All five control factors are marked `from_graph: False`: the run's node-type inventory contains mediators, mechanisms, variables, conditions and constructs but not a single confounder-typed node, so the graph contributed nothing to confounding control, and the Boolean is authored by the Designer rather than independently validated.

### 7.2 The counterfactual: h4 under causal-graph semantics

Suppose the pipeline had been in the business of producing a strict causal graph. The exercise is unusually clean because this domain is one of the narrow regimes flagged in Section 6 where the ladder is satisfiable: the "treatment" is a computational procedure, so interventions are exact, cheap and repeatable, and with algorithms and evaluation fixed there is no latent confounding worth the name. The outcome would differ in four structural ways. (i) *Variables, not concepts.* The nodes would be measurable quantities with units and domains: an exogenous matrix-level variable S (a spectral or normalised low-rank reconstruction-error summary of W), two intervention variables r (rank) and b (precision), an intermediate E(r, b, W) (reconstruction error), and outcome variables Y (perplexity delta, task-score delta at matched storage). "Inherent low-rank structure," a construct, becomes S, a statistic. (ii) *No coupling node.* The centre of h4, the reified "rank–precision error coupling," would not be a node at all. In a structural causal model an interaction is a property of the functional form, a non-additive term in E's structural equation, not a variable that can cause or be caused; it would be represented as a separate node only if explicitly defined as a derived variable with a domain, a measurement rule and intervention semantics. The claim graph reifies it because its ontology has only concepts and assertion edges, and the panel's `stretched` grade on the term is the claim-layer shadow of this type error. (iii) *Structural equations with intervention semantics.* The chain would become E = f(r, b, W) with an interaction term whose dependence on S is the hypothesis; "conditions" would resolve into effect modification (S moderates the b-effect on E), and "determines effectiveness of" into mediation of the outcome through E, both expressible in do-notation and estimable from the very factorial design the plan already specifies, since do(r, b) is literally executable here. (iv) *A quantitative, scoped terminus.* The final outcome would not be a status label but an estimate: an interaction coefficient with uncertainty, per stratum of S, over named checkpoints and layers. The current artifact ends where the causal version would begin: the plan is, almost verbatim, the identification strategy, and what is missing is execution, data, estimation and the typed variable layer to receive the estimate. Until the plan is executed and analysed, neither the difference-making nor the mechanistic evidence has been established <sup>[13](#reference-13)</sup>.

### 7.3 What the current output solves

Measured against the failure modes documented in Sections 4 and 5, the h4 artifact is a success of exactly the kind the architecture promises. It prevents invention-by-citation: the mechanism Greenberg documented, hypothesis transmuted into fact through repetition <sup>[28](#reference-28)</sup>, is structurally blocked, because the invented construct is marked by empty provenance, the panel audit records that CALDERA asserts no such coupling, and the reader card states "testable idea, not a proven conclusion." It refuses fabricated grounding: where a model pipeline would typically hallucinate a plausible methods citation, the committed plan carries `no_relevant_methods` and says so in rendered prose, with the runtime grounding rule operating in production. It converts a vague conjecture into a falsifiable protocol, with predicted directions, matched baselines, preregistration and refutation conditions, the difference-making half of the Russo–Williamson requirement packaged for a human executor with the mechanistic half held honestly as "vague." And it preserves recoverability: the post-run repair reconstructed four plans from final parseable Designer responses in the audit log through validated transactions without touching the event store, which is auditability under failure, the property transcript-based agent systems lack.

### 7.4 What it does not solve

The artifact equally displays the approach's present ceilings. Epistemically, h4 is a claim about a claim that no evidence has touched: both edges are unverified with null confidence, the run attached zero evidence links graph-wide, and the support-only scheduling means that even if verification had run, refuting evidence had no scheduled channel, in a domain where quantisation-error interactions are extensively measured and counterevidence plausibly exists. Representationally, the moderation structure is mis-typed: the interaction lives as a mechanism-typed node with free-text relations that no computation reads, polarity and effect size are absent, and the graph could not have expressed "S moderates the effect of b on Y" if the Synthesist had wanted to say exactly that. The graph contributed no experimental-design knowledge: with no confounder-typed nodes, all controls came from the model, so the advertised graph-to-plan channel was inert in this run. And the loop is open at both ends: upstream, no data plane could have tested the conjecture's premises against published measurements; downstream, the falsification clause will never fire inside the system, because nothing executes the plan or feeds a result back. The case thereby sharpens Section 6's conclusion: for h4's regime the missing rungs are not the impossible ones, since variables, interventions and sufficiency are all locally attainable, but precisely the unbuilt ones, typed quantitative content, a data plane, and execution with feedback.

## 8. Related systems

INDRA assembles mechanistic statements from literature and databases with grounding, deduplication and per-statement belief, and its EMMAA extension maintains self-updating disease models: the closest large-scale precedent, sharing the present system's core semantic decision that belief quantifies extraction and support correctness rather than causal truth, while lacking its transactional verdict machinery <sup>[45](#reference-45), [60](#reference-60)</sup>. SemMedDB <sup>[15](#reference-15)</sup> and SPOKE-class knowledge graphs <sup>[94](#reference-94)</sup> demonstrate the noise regime literature-scale assembly must survive; CauseNet does the same for the open web and adopts the "claimed relation" framing explicitly <sup>[16](#reference-16)</sup>. SciFact contributes the claim-verification task shape, supports and refutes with rationales <sup>[95](#reference-95)</sup>; nanopublications contribute the ontic–epistemic separation as a formalism <sup>[90](#reference-90)</sup>. Among agentic systems, POPPER <sup>[38](#reference-38)</sup> and Kosmos <sup>[39](#reference-39)</sup> bracket the present design from the validation and world-model sides, and Robin demonstrates the human-in-the-loop discovery loop the experiment stage anticipates <sup>[43](#reference-43)</sup>. GraphHypothesize's distinctive position is not any single component but the combination: claim-level semantics with INDRA-style provenance plus a transactional, deterministic settlement core with abstention, the auditability of a database with the epistemic caution of an evidence-synthesis protocol.

## 9. Limitations of this analysis

Five caveats bound the conclusions. First, quantitative figures cited from external papers were taken from the original sources as archived or retrieved during this analysis and were not independently reproduced. Second, the code discussion reflects the public checkout; configuration-dependent findings, notably the support-only verification schedule of Section 5.3, are claims about shipped defaults, not about what the architecture permits. Third, the rationale in Sections 4.6 and 4.8 synthesizes visible implementation choices, public docs and cited literature rather than reporting a single canonical statement. Fourth, the case study examines one hypothesis from one run; it illustrates the mechanisms established by the code rather than estimating their frequencies, and its domain is friendlier to interventionist reconstruction than most the pipeline may serve. Fifth, Section 4.8 characterises the hypothesis-generation systems' evaluation regimes from their papers and from the project's own evaluation plan, not from running them; whether the design produces better or more precise hypotheses is an empirical question the `fhq-v1` results have yet to answer.

## 10. Conclusion

A causal graph asserts what the world would do under intervention; a claim graph asserts what the literature says, and can prove it. Between those two objects stands the entire assumption base of structural causal modelling, well-defined variables and interventions, sufficiency, Markov and faithfulness, together with data to test it, none of which literature-fed extraction can supply, and no amount of claim aggregation, model judgment or benchmark-fragile discovery can currently substitute for it. GraphHypothesize's decision to build the second object and build it strictly, with deterministic verdict authority, five-gate transactions, provenance that refuses to guess and abstention that refuses to settle, is therefore the epistemically correct one, and the 1989–2026 literature, the field's flagship systems and the public implementation converge on it. The implementation's real debts are narrower than its representational choice: a verdict space presently starved to support-or-abstain by its retrieval schedule, an uncalibrated scalar confidence, and bias-blind evidence weighting. The h4 case study renders both halves of this verdict concrete: a production artifact that audits its own invented construct, refuses fabricated grounding and delivers a falsifiable preregistered design, yet whose edges no evidence has touched and whose experiment, in a domain where do() is literally executable, stops at prose. Against the field's hypothesis generators and their benchmarks, the defence of that decision is not that causal claims and deterministic settlement produce better hypotheses, since nothing yet shows they do, but that this pipeline commits state, measures literature support, emits experiments and moves resources, none of which a benchmarked idea generator does; whether the same machinery also helps on expert preference is what the `fhq-v1` comparison will show. The path forward is equally concrete: schedule the contradiction and confounder roles; type the causal content; ground the nodes; and then, context by context, climb the seven-rung ladder, keeping the claim graph beneath as the permanent, auditable record of what was asserted, by whom, on what evidence, so that every promotion from claim to cause is itself a claim the system can defend.
