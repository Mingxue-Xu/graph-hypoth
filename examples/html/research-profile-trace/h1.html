Research question: When does structured pruning reduce inference cost while preserving factual accuracy?

Proposed hypothesis h1

Prune the parts that are redundant for factual recall first.

Possible explanation

What might be happening: Some attention heads may duplicate routing information, while MLP channels may carry entity-specific facts that factual QA needs.

Why it matters: The result would turn structured pruning from a generic compression knob into a safer target-selection policy.

How to check: Run matched head-first and MLP-first ablations at the same sparsity and latency budgets, then compare factual QA accuracy.

What would change our mind: The idea weakens if MLP-first pruning preserves factuality equally well at matched latency.

Status: This is a testable idea, not a proven conclusion.

  • structured pruning — removing whole model components rather than individual weights (Example profile)
  • factual accuracy — whether answers preserve factual claims under evaluation (Example profile)
